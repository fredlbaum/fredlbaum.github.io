Hacker groups, security podcasts, and resources recommended by **K0FLB**.

## Local Hacker Groups

### [NoCo 2600](https://noco2600.org/)

Northern Colorado 2600 meeting. Security professionals and enthusiasts sharing knowledge, tools, and research monthly.

### [DC970](https://dc970.org/)

Northern Colorado DEF CON group (area code 970). Monthly meetups for the local security community.

## Security Podcasts

### [Darknet Diaries](https://darknetdiaries.com/)

Jack Rhysider's true stories from the dark side of the Internet: hackers, breaches, and cybercrime.

### [Security Now](https://twit.tv/shows/security-now)

Steve Gibson and Leo Laporte break down the week's security news and how the technology works. Full episode archive and transcripts at [GRC.com](https://www.grc.com/securitynow.htm).
