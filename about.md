Erick is a Network Security Engineer for the Thompson School District in Northern Colorado, where he specializes in enterprise infrastructure and network protection. A Wisconsin native raised in Colorado, he is heavily active in the regional infosec community in both the NoCO 2600 and DC970 groups. Outside of his professional systems work, he is an amateur radio operator (K0FLB), a retro gaming and computing enthusiast, and a passionate collector of diverse technical hobbies. He lives in Northern Colorado with his wife and two dogs.

> *"A towel, it says, is about the most massively useful thing an interstellar hitchhiker can have."*
