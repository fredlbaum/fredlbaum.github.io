> *"DON'T PANIC."*

The **Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy** is an indispensable companion for all interstellar travelers. It famously outsold the far more thorough *Encyclopedia Galactica* for two reasons: it's slightly cheaper, and it has the words **DON'T PANIC** printed in large, friendly letters on the cover.

This whole site is my small, Earth-bound tribute to it.

## Thank You, Douglas Adams

None of this would exist without **Douglas Adams** (1952–2001). He took a comedy radio show and turned it into a universe: a planet demolished for a hyperspace bypass, a depressed robot with a brain the size of a planet, a towel as the most useful thing you can carry, and the answer to life, the universe, and everything turning out to be **42**.

What makes his writing last is that the jokes are never just jokes. Under the silliness are sharp observations about bureaucracy, technology, and how strange it is to be a person. He was also a genuine tech enthusiast, an early fan of personal computers and the internet, who imagined a pocket guide to everything decades before most of us carried one.

So: thank you, Douglas. For the laughs, for the towel, and for teaching a lot of us not to panic.

## A Trilogy in Five Parts

The Guide has been told and retold in more forms than almost any other story:

* **1978:** It began as a BBC Radio 4 comedy series.
* **1979–1992:** It became five novels, cheerfully billed as "a trilogy in five parts": *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*, *The Restaurant at the End of the Universe*, *Life, the Universe and Everything*, *So Long, and Thanks for All the Fish*, and *Mostly Harmless*.
* **1981:** A BBC television series followed.
* **1984:** Adams co-wrote an Infocom text adventure of the same name, famous for being both very funny and very hard.
* **2005:** A feature film arrived, whose bright, animated Guide interface inspired this site's menu.

## Where the Guide Shows Up on This Site

* **The home menu:** the rainbow pill bars on a yellow screen nod to the Guide's look in the 2005 film.
* **THE GUIDE button:** the black pill with the red **DON'T PANIC** badge brings you here.
* **The tagline:** "Sub-Etha Transceiver" borrows the Guide's faster-than-light messaging network, which seemed right for a ham radio site.
* **The 404 page:** a missing page might get a Vogon demolition notice, Deep Thought's very long search, Marvin's service log, Vogon poetry, or a small text adventure where you'd better have your towel.

## A Few Things Every Hitchhiker Should Know

* **42** is the answer. The question is still being worked on.
* **Always know where your towel is.**
* **Towel Day** is **May 25th**, when fans carry a towel in Douglas Adams' memory.
* **Earth's entry** in the Guide was eventually expanded to two words: *Mostly Harmless*.
