> *"DON'T PANIC."*

The **Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy** is an indispensable companion for all interstellar travelers.

It has already supplanted the great *Encyclopedia Galactica* as the standard repository of all knowledge and wisdom, for two important reasons:
1. It is slightly cheaper;
2. It has the words **DON'T PANIC** inscribed in large, friendly letters on its cover.
