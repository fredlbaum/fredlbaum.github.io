404 (PAGE, MISSING)
Status: formerly "Harmless." Now "Not Here."

A page is a small, rectangular arrangement of words,
much like a planet but with fewer bypasses. This one
has wandered off, been demolished, or was never written
in the first place, which the Guide notes is by far the
most common fate of all pages.

Travelers arriving here are advised to check the
address they typed, return to the Interactive Menu,
and, above all, not to panic.

The Guide regrets any inconvenience, and also the
inconvenience of regretting it.
