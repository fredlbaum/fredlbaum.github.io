Welcome to the **K0FLB** equipment profile. Below is an overview of the current primary transceivers, antenna farm, portable field gear, and station accessories.

---

### <span id="transceivers">Transceivers & Rigs</span>
* **Primary HF Base Station**: High-performance multi-band HF transceiver operating 160m through 6m, covering SSB, CW, and digital modes.
* **VHF / UHF Base & Repeater Rig**: Dual-band FM and digital voice transceiver for local repeaters and simplex operations.

---

### <span id="antennas">Antenna Farm</span>
* **Multi-Band HF Wire**: Resonant dipole and end-fed half-wave (EFHW) configurations covering primary HF bands.
* **VHF / UHF Vertical**: High-gain collinear vertical mounted for omnidirectional regional coverage.

---

### <span id="portable">Portable & QRP</span>
* **Field Station**: Ultra-compact QRP transceiver setup for field operations, Parks on the Air (POTA), and emergency communications.
* **Rapid Deployment Wire**: Lightweight telescoping mast and portable link-dipole system.

---

### <span id="power">Power & Batteries</span>
* **Station Power**: Clean regulated switching power supply backed up by LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate) battery bank and solar charging controllers for off-grid resilience.

---

### <span id="accessories">Keys & Audio</span>
* **CW Keying**: Dual-lever iambic paddle with magnetic tension adjustment.
* **Audio & Processing**: Studio dynamic broadcast microphone with customized equalization.
